The cumulative distribution function of Student's t-distribution.

## Basic example

The cumulative distribution function of Student's t: the probability that a t statistic with `df` degrees of freedom falls below `t`. Zero always gives 0.5, and feeding a quantile back in returns its probability (`studentTCdf(2.57, 5)` is close to 0.975), since this is the inverse of `studentTQuantile`.
