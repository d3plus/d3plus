d3-time-format locale definitions (date and time patterns, period, day, and month names) keyed by locale code, used when formatting dates on axes, timelines, and tooltips.

## Basic example

Date and time definitions in the shape `d3-time-format`'s `timeFormatLocale` expects: the `%x`/`%X`/`%c` patterns, AM/PM periods, and day and month names. Axes and timelines use the entry matching the chart's `locale`; the preview formats one date three ways with the chosen entry.
