Creates a streamgraph from an array of data: a StackedArea whose layers
flow around a central baseline instead of rising from zero. Layers stack
with `stackOffset: "silhouette"`, which centers every stack on the same
line, ordered `"insideOut"` (the largest layers in the middle) along
smooth `monotoneX` curves. The value axis hides its labels and gridlines,
since a moving baseline has no zero to read them from; each layer's
thickness carries its value, and tooltips show each value and its share
of the stack. Set `stackOffset("wiggle")` to keep each layer's slope as
steady as it can instead, at the cost of a stream that drifts up or down
as its total changes.

## Basic example

Each `groupBy` series is a layer, stacked around a central baseline instead of rising from zero. The thickest layers sit in the middle, and each layer's thickness at a year is its value. The value axis shows no numbers, since the baseline moves; hover a layer for its value and its share of the stack.

## Wiggle

The default `stackOffset` of `"silhouette"` centers every year's stack on the same line, so the stream stays symmetric as its total changes. `"wiggle"` shifts the baseline to keep each layer's slope as steady as it can instead, which makes single layers easier to follow, while the stream drifts up or down as its total grows or shrinks.
