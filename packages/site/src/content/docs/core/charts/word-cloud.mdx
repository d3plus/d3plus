Draws one word per row, its font size set by its value (`size`, read from
`value` by default). The words are placed largest first, each walking a
spiral out from the middle of the chart until it lands clear of the words
before it. Collisions are tested against each word's actual glyphs, not its
box, so small words nest in the gaps of large ones. The same data always
gives the same layout. When the words don't all fit, every font size shrinks
together until they do. Hover a word to see its value and its share of every
word drawn, and scroll or use the zoom controls to read the small ones.

## Basic example

Each word's text is its `groupBy` id (or its `label`), and rows with the same
id add up. Font sizes run from `fontMin` to `fontMax`, by the square root of
the value by default (`fontScale`), so each word's ink area grows roughly in
step with its count.

## Colored by group

With two `groupBy` levels, the outer one colors the words and fills the
legend. Hover a legend entry to highlight its words, or click it to hide
them.

## Rotated words

`rotate` turns the words: a number turns every word, an array of angles
gives each word one of them (picked from its name, so it keeps its angle
across redraws), and a function `(d, i) => degrees` sets each word's own.
By default every word is horizontal.

<Playground chart="WordCloud" code={rotatedWords} controls={["rotate","spiral"]} />

## Color scale

`colorScale` colors each word by a second measure, here the share of its
mentions that come from positive reviews.

<Playground chart="WordCloud" code={colorScale} controls={["colorScale"]} />

## Threshold

`threshold` merges the words under that share of the total into one word,
named by `thresholdName` and drawn like any other, sized by their summed
value. Its tooltip lists the words it holds. It is off by default.

<Playground chart="WordCloud" code={threshold} controls={["threshold","thresholdName"]} />

## Small multiples

`facet` draws a cloud per part of speech. Each panel sizes its own words, so
compare words within a panel rather than across panels.
