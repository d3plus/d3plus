Creates a simple rows/columns Matrix view of any dataset.

## Basic example

Draws a cell at every `row` × `column` intersection (exporter vs. importer continent); `groupBy` supplies both dimensions and `colorScale` shades each cell by Trade Value.

## With color scale

Shades cells with a `colorScaleConfig` `scale: "jenks"` (natural-breaks) classification and moves the legend to the right via `colorScalePosition`.

<Playground chart="Matrix" code={withColorScale} controls={["colorScale","colorScaleConfig","colorScalePosition"]} />

## Dark theme color scales

On a dark theme (`"dark"`, or `"auto"` on a dark page) a color scale runs from a dim tint just above the background up to a bright blue, so low values recede and high values stand out, the mirror of the pale→dark ramp on light. Set `theme` to `"light"` to compare. With `colorScale: "change"`, a diverging scale, the two poles meet at a dark neutral instead of near-white, with bright red and blue at the ends. The theme's `colorScale` tokens (`max`, `mid`, `min`, `missing`) set these colors, and `colorScaleConfig.colorMax`/`colorMid`/`colorMin` or a `color` array still win.
