Creates a Pareto chart: one bar per category (`x`, or `y` with
`discrete("y")` for horizontal bars), sorted from the largest total to
the smallest, and a line of the running share of the total through each
category, on a 0–100% axis to the right of the bars (above horizontal
ones). A dashed reference line marks 80% (see `paretoThreshold`).
`threshold` folds the smallest categories into one bar, drawn last.
Tooltips show each bar's value, its share of the total, and the
cumulative share through its category.

## Basic example

The bars run from the most common cause to the least, and the line climbs through each bar's running share of every defect, reaching 100% at the last one. Where it crosses the dashed 80% line shows how few causes account for most of the defects.

<Playground chart="Pareto" code={basicExample} controls={["paretoThreshold"]} />

## Horizontal

`discrete("y")` lays the bars along the y axis, largest at the top, with the cumulative share on an axis across the top.

<Playground chart="Pareto" code={horizontal} controls={["discrete","x","y"]} />

## Moving the reference line

`paretoThreshold` moves the reference line to another share (here 70%), and `paretoThresholdConfig` styles it like a `referenceLines` entry: its `label`, `stroke`, `strokeOpacity`, `strokeWidth`, and `dash`. `paretoThreshold(false)` hides it.

<Playground chart="Pareto" code={referenceLine} controls={["paretoThreshold","paretoThresholdConfig"]} />

## Other

`threshold` folds every category under that share of the total into one bar, named by `thresholdName`. It is drawn last, whatever its size, so the line still runs from the largest category down, and its tooltip lists the categories it holds.

<Playground chart="Pareto" code={threshold} controls={["threshold","thresholdName"]} />

## Stacked series

With `groupBy` naming series inside each category and `stacked(true)`, each category's bar stacks its series, and categories are sorted, and the line accumulates, by the stack's total.

<Playground chart="Pareto" code={stackedSeries} controls={["stacked"]} />

## Small multiples

`facet` draws one Pareto chart per value. Each panel sorts its own categories and runs its own line, so panels fit their own scales by default; `facetConfig({scales: "shared"})` gives them one value axis.
