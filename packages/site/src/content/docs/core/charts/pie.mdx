Uses the d3 pie layout to create SVG arcs based on an array of data.

## Basic example

Each slice's angle is proportional to its `value` (Percentage), with one arc per `groupBy` category.

<Playground chart="Pie" code={basicExample} controls={["value"]} />

## Padded slices

`padPixel: 4` inserts a 4px gap between slices (converted to an angle from the radius); `padAngle` sets the same spacing directly in radians.

<Playground chart="Pie" code={paddedSlices} controls={["padPixel","padAngle"]} />

## Drill down on click

A hierarchical `groupBy` (["category", "id"]) renders the top level first; click a slice to drill into its children. A back button appears automatically to return.

<Playground chart="Pie" code={drillDownOnClick} controls={["depth","groupBy"]} />

## Background images

Set under a shape's name (`shapeConfig.Path.backgroundImage`), the image applies to that shape kind only. Each flag covers its wedge's bounding box and is clipped to the wedge's outline.

<Playground chart="Pie" code={backgroundImages} controls={["shapeConfig"]} />

## Quarter pie

`startAngle: 0, endAngle: 90` draws a quarter pie from 12 to 3 o'clock. A partial ring is sized to the part it draws and centered in the chart, so the quarter fills the chart's height. An `endAngle` smaller than `startAngle` lays the slices out counter-clockwise.

<Playground chart="Pie" code={quarterPie} controls={["startAngle","endAngle"]} />

## Slice labels

Each slice's name sits centered on the line through the middle of the slice, with its share on a smaller line beneath. The name takes the largest size that shows it whole inside the slice (up to `shapeConfig.labelConfig.fontMax`, 50px by default), wrapping between words where that reads larger, and the share is left off where it would shrink the name too much. A slice with room to spare keeps its label halfway between where the slice gets too narrow and its rim.

<Playground chart="Pie" code={sliceLabels} controls={["labelPosition","shapeConfig"]} />

## Labels outside the ring

A slice too small to show its whole name inside takes a label outside the ring instead, with its share, joined to it by a thin leader line. The labels stack in a column on the side each slice faces, and the ring shrinks just enough to leave them room. `labelPosition: "outside"` labels every slice this way, and `"inside"` keeps every label inside (a slice too small for one goes unlabeled). When there isn't room for them all, the smallest slices' labels are left out.

<Playground chart="Pie" code={outsideLabels} controls={["labelPosition"]} />
