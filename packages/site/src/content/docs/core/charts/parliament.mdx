Draws a parliament (arc-dot) chart: one dot per seat on concentric half
rings, colored by party (`groupBy`), each row's `sum` its seats. The parties
fill the arc from left to right in data order, each seat placed by its
angle so every party takes a wedge. The total sits in the middle
(`centerLabel`) and a dashed line marks a majority (`majority`).
Parliament is a [Waffle](/core/charts/waffle/) with `layout: "arc"`, one
cell per seat (`unit: 1`), so Waffle's options apply too.

## Basic example

List the parties in the order they sit, by convention from left to right across the political spectrum: the seats fill the arc in that order. The rings are as many as the house needs to seat everyone about as far apart along each ring as the rings are apart. Hover a seat to highlight its party, or click a legend entry to hide it and reseat the house.

<Playground chart="Parliament" code={basicExample} controls={["groupBy","sum","sort"]} />

## Majority

`majority` draws a dashed line across the rings at the seat a majority needs, and writes the count ("Majority: 226") in the middle, under the total (or across the middle when `centerLabel` is off): `true` (the default) for more than half of the house, or a number of seats for a supermajority, here two thirds. Seats fill from the left, so a block of parties from the left end that reaches past the line holds a majority. `false` hides it, and `centerLabel` sets or hides the total in the middle.

<Playground chart="Parliament" code={supermajority} controls={["majority","centerLabel"]} />

## Rings and sweep

`rings` sets the number of rings, `innerRadius` the size of the empty middle (pixels, or a function of the outer radius; 40% of it by default), and `startAngle` and `endAngle` the sweep, in degrees clockwise from 12 o'clock, as on a Pie: `-90` to `90` by default, a half circle.
