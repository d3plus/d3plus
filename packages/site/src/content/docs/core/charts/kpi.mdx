Creates KPI tiles from an array of data: one tile per row (or `groupBy`
group) showing its `value` large, its change against a previous value as
▲/▼ colored good or bad news, and with `time` set, a sparkline of its
history. The tiles flow left to right in a grid sized to the chart.

## Basic example

Give each metric a row per period and set `time`: each tile shows its latest period's value, the change from the period before it, and a sparkline of every period with the latest one marked. A rise reads as good news (green) and a fall as bad news (red). `valueFormat` formats the values and the absolute change; the percent change is written in the chart's locale.

<Playground chart="KPI" code={basicExample} controls={["sparkline","delta"]} />

## Good news and bad news

`deltaDirection: "down"` makes a fall the good news, for costs, churn, or wait times; a function sets it per tile. `delta` picks what the change shows: `"percent"` (the default), `"absolute"`, `"both"`, or `false`. A tile that didn't move shows its change in gray.

<Playground chart="KPI" code={goodNewsAndBadNews} controls={["delta","deltaDirection"]} />

## Previous values

Without `time`, give each row its own comparison: `previous` reads it from a key (`"previous"` by default) or a function. `comparisonLabel` sets the words after the change ("vs. previous" by default, translated), or `false` hides them; they're left out of a tile too narrow to hold them.

<Playground chart="KPI" code={previousValues} controls={["comparisonLabel","delta"]} />

## Sparklines and the grid

`sparkline` draws each tile's history as an `"area"` (the default), a `"line"`, or `"bar"`s with the current period in full color, or `false` for none. Every tile's sparkline takes one color, the color defaults' `sequential` blue, so it never reads as good or bad news; set `color` to color the tiles by a key. The grid picks the column count that draws the tiles largest at `tileConfig.aspect` (1.75 by default); `tileConfig.columns` or `rows` fixes it, `padding` spaces the tiles, and `fill`, `stroke`, `strokeWidth`, and `rx` style each tile's card. A tile too short for everything shrinks its text, then leaves out the comparison words, the label, and the delta, in that order, so nothing spills out of its card. On a right-to-left page (`dir="rtl"`), each tile's text starts at its right edge, with the comparison words to the left of the change.

<Playground chart="KPI" code={sparklines} controls={["sparkline","tileConfig"]} />

## Small multiples

`facet` draws a grid of tiles per panel, each tile's history and change read from that panel's rows.

<Playground chart="KPI" code={smallMultiples} controls={["facet"]} />

## Choosing the period

The sparklines show each tile's history, so the timeline is off by default. With `timeline: true`, picking a period shows its values, compared with the period before; brushing several periods sums them and compares the sum with as many periods before them.
