Creates a dumbbell (range dot) chart from an array of data with one row per
category and series, such as a country's value in 2010 and in 2020. Each
category, read from the axis named by `discrete` (`"y"` by default, for
horizontal dumbbells), draws a circle per row at its value, colored by
`groupBy`, with a connector between the category's smallest and largest
values. Circles are styled by `shapeConfig.Circle` and connectors by
`shapeConfig.Line`; `gapSort` orders the categories by the size of their
gap. `arrows` draws each connector as an arrow from the category's first
value to its last, and `connector: "bar"` draws a floating bar in place of
the dots.

## Basic example

Give the chart one row per category and series: here a country's life expectancy in 2000 and in 2021. `groupBy` names the series, which color the dots and fill the legend, and a connector spans each country's two values. Hover a country to compare both years in one tooltip.

## Sorted by gap

`gapSort` orders the categories by their gap, the distance between each one's smallest and largest value. `"descending"` puts the widest gap at the top; `"ascending"` the narrowest.

## Vertical ranges

Set `discrete` to `"x"` for vertical dumbbells. A category can hold any number of points; the connector spans its lowest to its highest.

<Playground chart="Dumbbell" code={verticalRanges} controls={["discrete"]} />

## Change arrows

`arrows: true` turns each connector into an arrow from the category's first value to its last, in data order: here from 2019 to 2023. The line stops at the arrowhead's base, and the head's tip stops just short of the later dot. Arrows are colored by direction, `increaseColor` (green by default) when the value rises and `decreaseColor` (red) when it falls, and the tooltip adds each country's change. `arrowSize` sets the head's length; a change too small to fit a head between the dots draws as a plain connector.

<Playground chart="Dumbbell" code={changeArrows} controls={["arrows","arrowSize","increaseColor","decreaseColor"]} />

## Change bars

`connector: "bar"` draws a thick floating bar between each category's values in place of the dots. With `arrows`, the bar runs from the first value and ends in an arrowhead at the last, colored by direction. The bars are 60% of the space between categories, at most 40 pixels thick; `connectorWidth` sets their thickness, and `shapeConfig.Rect` styles them.

<Playground chart="Dumbbell" code={changeBars} controls={["connector","arrows","connectorWidth"]} />

## Span chart

Without `arrows`, a bar connector spans each category's smallest to largest value, as a span (range) chart: here each month's low and high temperature. The tooltip adds each month's range. The legend is hidden, since the bars draw in place of the series' dots.

<Playground chart="Dumbbell" code={spanBars} controls={["connector","connectorWidth"]} />

## Small multiples

`facet` draws a panel per continent. With independent scales, each panel lists only its own countries, sorted by its own gaps.
