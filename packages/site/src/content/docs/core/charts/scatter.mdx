Creates a scatter plot from an array of data: each row draws as a point at
its `x` and `y` values, on continuous axes (set `discrete` to place points
along a category axis). Points are `sizeMin` pixels in radius; a `size`
accessor scales them between `sizeMin` and `sizeMax` and adds a size
legend (see BubbleChart for bubbles sized by area). Hover any point for
its values.

## Basic example

Each row draws as a point at its `x` and `y` values, on two continuous axes. Grouping by `species` first colors the points by species and adds a legend; hover any point for its values.

## Sized points

A `size` accessor scales each point's radius between `sizeMin` and `sizeMax` and adds a size legend. To size points by area, so a value twice as large draws twice the area, use BubbleChart.

<Playground chart="Scatter" code={sizedPoints} controls={["size","sizeMin","sizeMax"]} />

## Trend line

Set `trendLine: true` to fit a least-squares line to each series (here each `species`), drawn behind the points. Hover a line for its equation and R².

<Playground chart="Scatter" code={trendLine} controls={["trendLine","trendLineConfig"]} />

## Point labels

Points too small to hold their label get it beside them instead, on the side facing the most open room (the point's Voronoi cell), where it overlaps no other point or label and stays inside the plot. Where the points crowd together there is no such room, so those labels hide; hover a point for its name. `labelPosition: "inside"` keeps labels inside the points, and `pointLabelConfig` sets the size and style of the labels beside them.
