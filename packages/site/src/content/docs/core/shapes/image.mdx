Creates SVG images based on an array of data.

## Basic example

A single datum places one image at its `x`/`y` at the given `width`/`height`; the `url` accessor here points to an inline SVG data URI.

<Playground chart="Image" code={basicExample} controls={["url"]} />

## Multiple images

One image per datum, each sized by its own `width` and `height` and centered on its `x`/`y`; `opacity` fades the third. Geomap and Network use this shape for icons and flags.

<Playground chart="Image" code={multipleImages} controls={["width","height","opacity"]} />

## Fitting the box

`preserveAspectRatio` sets how an image whose shape differs from its box fits it, as an SVG `preserveAspectRatio` value, the same on SVG and Canvas. Unset, the whole image sits centered inside the box (`"xMidYMid meet"`); an alignment with `slice` covers the box and crops the picture on the sides the alignment leaves out, here keeping its left edge; and `"none"` stretches it.
