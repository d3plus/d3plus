## Text wrapping

A `label` on a Circle now wraps to the circle's interior: each line is only as wide as the circle is at that height, so text fills the round shape — widest through the middle, shorter at the top and bottom — and auto-sizes to fit. Previously labels were confined to a square inscribed in the circle, wasting the rounded sides. Just set a `label` accessor; d3plus handles the rest.
