Creates SVG areas based on an array of data.

## Basic example

The six points share one `id`, so they join into a single filled area; `fillOpacity` renders it as a translucent band, and the `curve` control changes how the vertices are interpolated.

<Playground chart="Area" code={basicExample} controls={["curve"]} />

## Band

Setting both `y0` and `y1` draws a band between two edges instead of filling down to a baseline, which is how confidence intervals and min/max envelopes are drawn behind a line.

<Playground chart="Area" code={band} controls={["y0","y1"]} />

## Curve and series

Each distinct `id` becomes its own area, and `curve` picks the d3 interpolation between points; `monotoneX` rounds the corners without overshooting the data.

<Playground chart="Area" code={curveAndSeries} controls={["curve","fill"]} />
