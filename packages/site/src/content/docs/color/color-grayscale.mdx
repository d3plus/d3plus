Strips a color's saturation while keeping its lightness and alpha, so a
de-emphasized mark keeps the contrast it had (and a transparent fill stays
transparent). Values d3-color can't parse, such as `"none"`, are returned
unchanged.

## Basic example

Removes a color's saturation but keeps its lightness and alpha, so each gray is as light as the color it replaces. Charts use it to fade the marks outside a highlight while their labels keep the contrast they were given.
