Formats a whole number as an ordinal for the given locale (`1` to `"1st"` in English, `"1.º"` in Spanish, `"1º"` in Portuguese, `"第1"` in Chinese). BumpChart's tooltips use it to read each rank.

## Basic example

English picks its suffix by the number's ordinal plural category, so `11` reads as `11th` but `22` as `22nd`; other locales take their own form.
